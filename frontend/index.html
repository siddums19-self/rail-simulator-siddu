🚀 Quick Start — How to Drive in 4 Steps
1. Ready the Loco: Press R (Release Brakes) and ensure P (Panto: Up) is raised on electric locos. Wait for the Green/Yellow starter signal & Station Master green flag.
2. Accelerate & Cruise: Press W (Throttle +) to build speed; press K (Cruise) to hold your current speed automatically. Press S (Throttle −) to reduce power or engage Reverse at 0 km/h.
3. Watch Signals & Crossings: Keep an eye on the Next Signal panel (top-right) and press H (Horn) before level crossings (W/L boards).
4. Stop Smoothly: Press Space (Brake) well before a RED signal or station stop board, then press R once halted.
